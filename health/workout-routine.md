# Workout Routine — 4-Day Back/Glutes/Core (InBody-Optimized)

**Goal:** Reach 45 kg, build core, back, and glutes  
**Experience:** Beginner  
**Equipment:** Full commercial gym  
**Based on:** InBody scan 2026-07-14 (50.5 kg, 20.6 kg SMM, 26% PBF)  
**Schedule:** Flexible 4 days/week (e.g. Tue / Wed / Fri / Sat)

Updated in Hevy on 2026-07-14. Starting weights added from Jul 14 gym session + estimates.

## Why this split
- **Back & core** trained twice/week (3 back exercises + 2 core per upper day)
- **Glutes** hit hard both lower days (hip thrust 4 sets, kickbacks, pull-throughs)
- **Chest volume reduced** — maintenance only via rows/face pulls, not a priority
- **Fat loss to 45 kg** comes from nutrition; this routine preserves/builds muscle during deficit

## Progression
- Add **2.5 kg** when you hit the top of the rep range for all sets
- Hip thrusts are your #1 glute builder — prioritize these
- Glutes & legs day weights are estimates — adjust up/down after first session
- Re-scan InBody every 4–6 weeks to track SMM and PBF
